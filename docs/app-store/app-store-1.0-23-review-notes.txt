Sofatap 1.0 (23) is a free manual touchpad and text-input remote for the user's computer. No account, demo login, password, subscription or purchase is required. It does not stream the screen or run autonomous AI tasks.

EQUIPMENT AND RECEIVER
Use an iPhone/iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17+ and an awake, unlocked computer on the same reachable home network. Install receiver 0.8.1 beta.1:
https://github.com/chekusu/sofatap/releases/tag/v0.8.1-beta.1
Downloads and setup:
https://github.com/chekusu/sofatap/blob/main/docs/app-store/support.md
Windows: extract the complete ZIP and run SofaTap.exe. Allow the receiver on a trusted private network if prompted; keep the firewall enabled.
Mac: choose Apple Silicon or Intel, move the signed/notarized Sofatap Receiver.app to Applications and grant it Accessibility permission. Requires macOS 15+.
Linux: follow the GTK/input dependency guide; Wayland is experimental. Receiver 0.7.2 beta.1 also supports standard local-network control. Mac peer transport is optional.

PAIR AND NAME
1. Open the receiver's native window and choose a local-network address reachable by the phone.
2. In Sofatap, tap the top-left device selector, then Scan QR Code under Add Device. Allow Camera and Local Network access, then scan. A complete pairing link can be pasted instead.
3. After authentication, keep the suggested name or enter another and save. My Devices supports switching, renaming, disconnecting and removing a pairing. The question mark opens the guide.

FUNCTIONAL TEST
1. Open a blank document on the computer. With the default Sofatap gesture preset, slide to move, tap for a left click, double-tap the left half for two left clicks or the right half for two right clicks. Hold 0.3 seconds, then move to drag; lift to release. Two fingers scroll naturally; dragging the right-edge strip down scrolls down.
2. The gear opens gesture settings: three presets, individual assignments and local practice. Computer and TV preferences are separate. The guide reflects current assignments. Build 23 tolerates a small resting finger roll during a hold; desktop volume and pointer-speed controls are not included.
3. Focus a writable native text field and open the phone keyboard. Supported Windows/Mac fields import text and synchronize typing, Backspace, selection and deletion without Send. Other fields, Linux and older receivers use review-and-send. No extra Return is appended; included line breaks may act as Enter in the destination app.
4. Tap the microphone, allow Microphone/Speech access, and tap again to stop. Built-in dictation needs supported on-device recognition; otherwise use the keyboard. Live fields synchronize recognized text; drafts require Send. Check the computer before retrying uncertain delivery.
5. Keep Sofatap foreground while restarting the receiver. The selected saved computer reconnects when its address and identity remain valid. Unconfirmed input is not replayed.

TV PREVIEWS AND PRIVACY
TV adapters are experimental; no TV model or third-party app is qualified. Android/Google TV needs its separate receiver and user-granted Accessibility for conditional overlay-pointer actions, not global mouse injection. Native navigation requires separate TV Remote Service pairing. Optional text/dictation needs the enabled, selected Sofatap keyboard and a supported Android editor, with explicit Send. LG webOS uses manual IP entry, certificate approval and TV authorization; TV typing is unavailable there. Neither adapter supports right-click or drag. Computer control above is the primary review path.
Pairings stay in device-only Keychain. Input uses pinned TLS. Built-in speech stays on the phone. Live editing transfers the supported field's text/selection; protected or read-only fields cannot use it. Backgrounding stops recording and disconnects. Login/elevated screens, PIN unlocking, Bluetooth and paid automation are unavailable.
