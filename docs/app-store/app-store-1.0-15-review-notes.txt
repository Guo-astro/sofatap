Sofatap 1.0 (15) is a free manual touchpad and text-input remote for a user's own computer on a home network. It does not stream the computer screen or run autonomous AI tasks. No Sofatap account, demo login, account password, subscription or purchase is required.

EQUIPMENT AND RECEIVER
Use an iPhone with iOS 17+ and an awake, unlocked computer on the same reachable local network. Install receiver 0.7.1 beta.1:
https://github.com/chekusu/sofatap/releases/tag/v0.7.1-beta.1
Direct download links and installation instructions:
https://github.com/chekusu/sofatap/blob/main/docs/app-store/support.md
Windows: extract the complete ZIP and run SofaTap.exe. Allow its receiver on a trusted private network if prompted; keep the firewall enabled.
Mac: choose Apple Silicon or Intel, move the signed/notarized Sofatap Receiver.app to Applications and grant Accessibility permission. Requires macOS 15+.
Linux: install the guide's GTK/input dependencies; support depends on the desktop. Wayland is experimental.

PAIR AND NAME
1. Open the receiver's native window and choose a local-network address reachable by the iPhone.
2. Open Sofatap. Tap the device selector at the top left, then Scan QR Code under Add Device. The camera is above the supported-platform information; no device-type selection is required.
3. Allow Camera and Local Network access, then scan. A complete pairing link can be pasted instead. Receiver 0.7.1 identifies its platform and name; the phone still validates its pinned certificate and pairing credential.
4. After authentication, keep the suggested device name or enter another and save. My Devices supports switching, renaming, disconnecting and removing a local pairing. Renaming does not rename the computer; removal affects only this iPhone. The top-right question mark opens the guide.

FUNCTIONAL TEST
1. Open a blank document on the computer. Slide one finger to move the pointer, tap to click, double-tap for right click, and hold 0.3 seconds before moving to drag. Two fingers move content naturally; the strip inside the touchpad's right edge scrolls down when dragged down.
2. Focus a writable native text field and open the phone keyboard. Supported Windows/Mac fields import existing text and synchronize typing, Backspace, selection and deletion without Send. Unsupported fields, including many browser and custom controls, use review-and-send; Linux and older receivers use this mode. Sofatap does not append an extra Return after your text. Line breaks you include may be handled as Enter by the destination app.
3. Tap the microphone and allow Microphone/Speech access. Built-in dictation requires supported on-device recognition; otherwise use the keyboard. Tap again to stop. Recognized text follows the same live/draft behavior. In draft mode, send text, append more, then send again; only the added suffix should arrive. Check the computer before retrying uncertain delivery.

TV DEVELOPMENT PREVIEWS
The app also includes explicitly experimental adapters. No physical TV model or third-party TV app is qualified. Android/Google TV needs its development receiver and user-enabled Accessibility service; its overlay pointer activates exposed accessible controls, not global mouse injection. LG webOS uses manual IP entry, certificate approval and TV authorization. TV typing, dictation, right-click and drag are unavailable. The computer path above is the release's primary review path.

PRIVACY AND LIMITS
Pairings remain in device-only Keychain. Input travels directly over pinned TLS. Built-in speech stays on the iPhone. Live editing transfers the supported field's full text and selection; protected/read-only fields cannot start it. The receiver cannot control login or elevated permission screens. Backgrounding stops recording and disconnects control. Bluetooth, PIN unlocking and paid AI automation are unavailable.
