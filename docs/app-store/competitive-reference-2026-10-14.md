## Direction for Sofatap

Use our own **暮色薄荷 / Mint Calm** charcoal and mint identity. Place one short benefit above a large, readable capture of the real app. Keep the first three images understandable at search-result size. Borrow the presentation pattern, not competitor wording, artwork, colors, device compositions or badges. The following is a proposed sequence, subject to capture and feature verification:

1. **“Your computer. From the sofa.”** Show the distinctive touch surface and a real connected test device. Make the phone-as-controller purpose immediately clear.
2. **“Type naturally. Or dictate.”** Show the actual composer with harmless sample text. Demonstrate the behavior supported by the pictured receiver; avoid a universal real-time-input promise.
3. **“Scan. Name. Connect.”** Show genuine pairing or naming UI. Never publish an active pairing credential, private address or token. Prefer a post-scan naming state; any QR footage needs an expired test credential and review before export.
4. **“Know which device you're controlling.”** Show actual saved-device management and the active-device indicator with fictional, descriptive names.
5. **“Familiar gestures. One touch surface.”** Use the app's actual guide or touch feedback, with a restrained explanatory gesture overlay if useful.

Use the same claim hierarchy in English, Chinese and Japanese, adjusting line breaks for each language. Capture current UI before designing the frame. Do not turn TV feasibility work, paid voice automation, lock-screen unlocking or unverified receiver compatibility into released-feature claims. Dictation remains basic text input and must not imply autonomous AI operation. Receiver requirements and platform limitations belong in clear supporting copy.

## Apple requirements and production boundaries

- Screenshots must show the app in use. Explanatory text and image overlays are permitted; the underlying interface must be authentic. Metadata must accurately represent available features. Use authorized material and fictional personal information. See [App Review Guidelines 2.3.1, 2.3.3 and 2.3.9](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata).
- App Previews use screen recordings of the app itself. Narration and explanatory overlays are allowed. Apple advises against filming people using a device or over-the-shoulder shots in an App Preview. A separate website film can serve a different purpose. See [Guideline 2.3.4](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata) and [App Previews](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/).
- Apple supports up to 10 screenshots and up to three previews per language. Previews can run up to 30 seconds and autoplay muted. The opening images, opening seconds and poster frame therefore need to communicate without sound. See [Creating your product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/).

For Sofatap, build a **20–25 second** preview from real app recordings: touch feedback, composer, pairing/naming and device management. This duration is a creative recommendation, not an Apple minimum. Keep any external computer demonstration in separate promotional material. Image generation may support exploration or decorative brand assets; it must not fabricate app UI, a successful connection, scan results or functional evidence. Check [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications) and [preview specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications) against the actual upload slots before final export.

Competitor source images remain private research references and are not included in Sofatap's distributable assets. Final acceptance requires visual review at phone size, genuine capture provenance, language review, removal of private data and successful validation of the upload files. This research alone does not establish App Store approval or asset performance.
