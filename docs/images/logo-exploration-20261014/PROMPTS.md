## 1. 柔和剪影 — 01-soft-silhouette

Use case: logo-brand. Create one production-quality square iOS app icon candidate for Sofatap, a phone touch remote for living-room computers and TVs. One unified front-facing emblem: a vertical smartphone becomes the tall back cushion of a friendly sofa, a wide softly rounded seat and two short arms cradle the phone. Inside the phone a single bold dark cursor arrow points up-left. Make the phone distinctly recognizable with one tiny earpiece cutout, but keep the silhouette beautifully simple. Design direction: flat vector-friendly filled silhouette, extraordinarily clean organic geometry, generous breathing room, pale luminous mint emblem (#A4F4CF) on uninterrupted deep ink teal background (#091316). Subtle restrained soft halo only, no 3D bevels. Emblem takes roughly 68 percent of square width and 66 percent height, centered, consistent rounded corners and thick readable shapes. Full-bleed square image, no outer rounded-corner frame, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no scene, no decorative particles. Crisp and identifiable at 32 pixels.
