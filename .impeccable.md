## Design Context

### Users
People relaxing on a sofa who control a living-room Windows, Mac or Linux computer from an iPhone. They should not need technical knowledge or a separate keyboard and mouse.

### Brand Personality
Playful, tactile, effortless. The creator explicitly wants a striking, cool particle response to touch while keeping the everyday interface very simple. The brand is Sofatap, one word.

### Aesthetic Direction
One generous rounded touch surface contains the existing logo, pairing/scan icon, bottom microphone and left keyboard icon. The resting surface has no explanatory copy or conventional mouse-pointer graphic. Keep the established dark and mint brand. Distinctive light and particles should come from the user's gestures, rather than continuous background decoration. The five desktop visual concepts remain unselected.

### Design Principles
1. The finger is the visual source: local touch trails and pulses never pretend to show the desktop cursor's absolute position.
2. Slide, tap, double tap and 300 ms hold-to-drag each get distinct feedback without changing gesture timing.
3. Keep primary controls within the same surface; reveal draft text, status and help only when requested or necessary.
4. Particle work is bounded and stops when idle or inactive. Respect Reduce Motion and preserve spoken labels, control hit targets and explicit recording state.
5. Verify the native implementation with isolated simulator evidence. Never treat visual previews as physical-phone input acceptance.
