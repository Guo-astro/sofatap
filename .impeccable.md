## Design Context

### Users
People relaxing on a sofa who control a living-room Windows, Mac or Linux computer from an iPhone. They should not need technical knowledge or a separate keyboard and mouse.

### Brand Personality
Playful, tactile, effortless. The creator explicitly wants a striking, cool particle response to touch while keeping the everyday interface very simple. The brand is Sofatap, one word.

### Aesthetic Direction
One generous rounded touch surface contains the existing logo, pairing/scan icon, bottom microphone and left keyboard icon. The resting surface has no explanatory copy or separate mouse-pointer graphic. Keep the established dark and mint brand. The entire surface is a softly moving dot matrix, not an empty canvas. Touch bends and displaces nearby dots, which settle back after release; single and double taps retain distinct ripples. The five desktop visual concepts remain unselected.

### Design Principles
1. The ambient field and local touch distortion never pretend to show the desktop cursor's absolute position.
2. Slide, tap, double tap and 300 ms hold-to-drag each get distinct feedback without changing gesture timing.
3. Keep primary controls within the same surface; reveal draft text, status and help only when requested or necessary.
4. Dot-field work is bounded: ambient movement is slow while active; inactivity and loss of control stop animation. Reduce Motion keeps the grid static. Respect Reduce Motion and preserve spoken labels, control hit targets and explicit recording state.
5. Verify the native implementation with isolated simulator evidence. Never treat visual previews as physical-phone input acceptance.
